Military Lab Tools

Military Lab Tools is a private, internal tool used by the owner of four YouTube channels:

  • Military Aero Lab
  • Military Space Lab
  • Military Ground Lab
  • Military Naval Lab

The channels publish public-domain U.S. military footage from DVIDS. The tool uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, only on these channels' own accounts, to:

  • read comments on the channels' videos and post replies written by the channel owner;
  • upload and schedule the channels' own videos;
  • read the channels' own view and subscriber statistics.

It is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.
