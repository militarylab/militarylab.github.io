Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Military Lab Tools ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Military Aero Lab, Military Space Lab, Military Ground Lab and Military Naval Lab YouTube channels to manage those channels.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner signs in, with the four channels' own Google accounts. The tool is not offered to anyone else.

What data the tool accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission, the tool accesses:

  • the channels' own videos, playlists and upload settings;
  • public comments posted on the channels' videos, including commenters' public display names;
  • the channels' own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the channels' videos, to read and reply to comments, and to review the channels' performance. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and working data are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is stored on any server operated by the tool.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.
